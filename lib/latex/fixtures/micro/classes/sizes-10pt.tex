% covers: every size command \tiny … \Huge in article [10pt]: font, \baselineskip between sizes, \parskip between paragraphs

\documentclass[10pt]{article}
\pagestyle{empty}
\begin{document}
{\tiny The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.\par}
{\scriptsize The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.\par}
{\footnotesize The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.\par}
{\small The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.\par}
{\normalsize The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.\par}
{\large The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.\par}
{\Large The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.\par}
{\LARGE The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.\par}
{\huge The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.\par}
{\Huge The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.\par}

Back at the normal size, a longer paragraph runs over more than one line so that the baselines inside it and the parskip before it are both measured against what TeX does with the same text.

{\small A small paragraph after it, long enough to break into two lines, checks the interline glue that the small size sets, including its own baselineskip.\par}
\end{document}
