\section{Greatest common divisor}
Euclid's algorithm replaces the larger number by its remainder modulo the smaller one until the remainder vanishes.
\begin{algorithm}[H]
\caption{Euclid's algorithm}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Function{Gcd}{$a, b$}
  \While{$b \ne 0$}
    \State $(a, b) \gets (b, a - qb)$
  \EndWhile
  \State \Return $a$
\EndFunction
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}
Each step at least halves one of the numbers every two steps, so the loop runs a logarithmic number of times.
