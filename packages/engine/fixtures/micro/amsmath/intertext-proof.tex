\begin{proof}
Let $\varepsilon>0$ be given. Then, by the triangle inequality,
\begin{align}
|f(x)-f(y)| &\le |f(x)-f_n(x)| + |f_n(x)-f_n(y)| \label{eq:tri} \\
\intertext{and, since the convergence in \eqref{eq:tri} is uniform,}
 &< \varepsilon \notag
\end{align}
for all $n$ large enough.
\end{proof}
