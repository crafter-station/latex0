\section{Summary}
The parish archive holds over 4000 documents dating from 1640 to 1950, including registers of baptisms, marriages and burials, vestry minutes and estate maps. Many are fragile.

\section{Objectives}
\begin{enumerate}

  \item Create a searchable online catalogue.
\end{enumerate}
