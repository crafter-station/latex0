Half of it is $\frac{1}{2}$ and a third is $\frac{a+b}{c}$, while
$\sum_{i=1}^n a_i$ and $\prod_{k=0}^{m} x_k$ keep their limits at the side in
text style. The integral $\int_0^\infty f(x)$ never takes limits, but
$\sum\limits_{i=1}^n i$ does. Nested fractions such as $\frac{\frac{1}{x}}{y}$
shrink to script style.
