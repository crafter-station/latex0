A tall formula such as $\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{n} i=\frac{n(n+1)}{2}$ in
running text pushes the lines apart, since the baselines cannot stay at their
usual distance. The same holds for $\displaystyle\prod_{k=1}^{m}\frac{1}{k}$
and for $\displaystyle\int_0^1 x^2$, while $\scriptstyle a+b$ is small.
