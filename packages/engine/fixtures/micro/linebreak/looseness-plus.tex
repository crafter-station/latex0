\tolerance=10000 \looseness=1 Typesetting is the composition of text by means of arranging physical type or its digital equivalents. Stored letters and other symbols are retrieved and ordered according to the orthography of a language for visual display. The breaking of paragraphs into lines is a small part of this craft, but it decides how evenly the words are spread and how easily the eye moves from one line to the next.
