% fixture: cite/option-nobreak
% tier: T3
% engine: document
% covers: cite.sty's nobreak option: \citeprepenalty, \citemidpenalty and \citepunctpenalty are \@M, so no line breaks before or in a citation
\documentclass{article}
\usepackage[nobreak]{cite}
\begin{document}
With the nobreak option a paragraph cannot be broken before a citation or inside one, so the lines of this paragraph are broken elsewhere than with the defaults \cite[see the long note here]{a,c,e} and also here \cite{b,a,f,e,d}, which is seen where a citation such as \cite{a,c,e} comes near the end of a line of the text.

\begin{thebibliography}{9}
\bibitem{a} One.
\bibitem{b} Two.
\bibitem{c} Three.
\bibitem{d} Four.
\bibitem{e} Five.
\bibitem{f} Six.
\end{thebibliography}
\end{document}
