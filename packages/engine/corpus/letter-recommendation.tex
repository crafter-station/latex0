To the Admissions Committee:

It is my pleasure to recommend Ms.\ Hannah Lee for admission to your doctoral program
in Mechanical Engineering. I have known Hannah for three years, first as a student in my
Fluid Mechanics course, where she earned the highest grade in a class of 94, and then as
an undergraduate researcher in my laboratory.

In the lab, Hannah designed and built a low-cost particle image velocimetry rig that is
now used by two graduate students. She taught herself the necessary optics and image
processing, wrote clean and well-documented analysis code, and presented her results at
the regional ASME student conference, where she won second prize.

Hannah is exceptionally independent, but she also collaborates generously. She is among
the top three undergraduates I have supervised in fifteen years of teaching. I recommend
her without reservation.
