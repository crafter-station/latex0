\section{Summary}
The parish archive holds over 4\,000 documents dating from 1640 to 1950, including
registers of baptisms, marriages and burials, vestry minutes and estate maps. Many are
fragile. We request \pounds 24\,500 to digitise and catalogue the collection and make it
freely available online.

\section{Objectives}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Conserve and digitise all registers and vestry minutes.
  \item Create a searchable online catalogue.
  \item Train ten volunteers in archival handling and metadata.
\end{enumerate}
