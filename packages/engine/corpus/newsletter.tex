\section*{Harvest Festival a Success}
More than 300 neighbours joined us on 20 September for the annual harvest festival. The
pumpkin-weighing contest was won by the Patel family with a 41\,kg giant, and the
children's scarecrow workshop produced eighteen remarkable creations.

for the new greenhouse fund.

\section*{Composting Corner}
Autumn leaves are a gardener's gold. Collect them in a wire cage or a bin bag with
holes, keep them moist, and within a year you will have leaf mould, an excellent soil
conditioner.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Do add: leaves, vegetable peelings, coffee grounds.
  \item Do not add: meat, dairy, diseased plants.
\end{itemize}
