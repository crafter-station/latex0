\section{Message from the CEO}
This year we cut emissions per tonne-kilometre by 9\% while growing volumes by 4\%. We
remain on track for our 2030 target of a 45\% reduction against the 2019 baseline.

\section{Emissions}
\subsection{Scope 1 and 2}
Direct emissions from our fleet fell to 182\,kt CO\textsubscript{2}e, mainly due to the
introduction of 140 electric vans in urban depots.

\subsection{Scope 3}
Purchased transport remains our largest source. We have begun requiring carbon data from
all subcontractors with more than 50 vehicles.

\section{People}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Lost-time injury rate: 0.42 per 200\,000 hours (2025: 0.51).
  \item Women in management: 31\% (2025: 28\%).
  \item Training hours per employee: 22.
\end{itemize}
